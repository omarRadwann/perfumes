The Atelier

Composed slowly,
bottled by hand.

Every ÉTHEREAL begins as a short list of rare materials — saffron, oud, ambergris, orris — sourced for character rather than yield, and held until the season is right.

We compose in extrait and eau de parfum, never diluted to fill a bottle. Each batch is small, finished by hand, and numbered — a little architecture of scent, and a little restraint.

6 Compositions · Eau de Parfum & Extrait

ÉTHEREAL — Awaken the Senses

Scent, made visible. Six fragrances, six worlds — composed in eau de parfum and extrait.

Noir Solaire

Amber / Oriental · Eau de Parfum · $245 · 50 ml, 100 ml

A sun that refuses to set.

Resin and gold held at the last hour of light. Saffron strikes against Bulgarian rose; amber, oud and bourbon vanilla draw the warmth down into something eternal. Worn close, it never quite goes dark.

Top notes: Bergamot, Pink Pepper, Saffron. Heart notes: Bulgarian Rose, Orris, Immortelle. Base notes: Amber, Oud, Bourbon Vanilla, Labdanum.

Verdane

Green / Floral · Eau de Parfum · $220 · 50 ml, 100 ml

The first green hour after rain, kept in glass.

Galbanum and crushed violet leaf open cold and wet. Fig and jasmine sambac soften the green; vetiver and oakmoss root it to the earth. A garden kept in glass, just after the storm.

Top notes: Galbanum, Violet Leaf, Bergamot. Heart notes: Fig, Jasmine Sambac, Tuberose. Base notes: Vetiver, Cedar, Oakmoss.

Cobalt Hour

Aquatic / Woody · Eau de Parfum · $230 · 50 ml, 100 ml

The minute the sea turns from blue to black.

Sea salt and yuzu over cold juniper. Iris and lavender hold the surface still while ambergris and driftwood pull toward the deep. The exact blue of dusk on water, made wearable.

Top notes: Sea Salt, Yuzu, Juniper. Heart notes: Iris, Lavender, Geranium. Base notes: Ambergris, Driftwood, White Musk.

Améthyste

Gourmand / Floral · Eau de Parfum · $235 · 50 ml, 100 ml

A confession written in velvet.

Black currant and plum bleed into violet and heliotrope. Beneath, cacao, tonka and sandalwood turn the floral carnal. Soft, dark and close to the skin — a secret you can almost hear.

Top notes: Black Currant, Plum, Bergamot. Heart notes: Violet, Heliotrope, Rose. Base notes: Cacao, Tonka, Sandalwood.

Blanc Absolu

White Floral / Clean · Eau de Parfum · $215 · 50 ml, 100 ml

Light, before it becomes color.

Aldehydes and neroli flare bright and clean. Orange blossom, ylang and gardenia open like a held breath; white amber and musk leave only a luminous trace. The colour of early light, worn as skin.

Top notes: Aldehydes, Neroli, Petitgrain. Heart notes: Orange Blossom, Ylang-Ylang, Gardenia. Base notes: White Amber, Musk, Sandalwood.

Fumée Rare

Smoky / Leather · Extrait de Parfum · $260 · 50 ml, 100 ml

Smoke that remembers the fire.

Incense and cardamom rise over leather and birch tar. Guaiac wood, vetiver and amber smoulder underneath — dry, dark and slow. The last grey curl of smoke after the flame is gone. Composed in extrait, never diluted.

Top notes: Incense, Cardamom, Pink Pepper. Heart notes: Leather, Birch Tar, Violet. Base notes: Guaiac Wood, Vetiver, Amber.
